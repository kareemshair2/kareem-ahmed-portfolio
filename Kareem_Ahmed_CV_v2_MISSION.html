01 / AboutProfile · Summary

Data into decisions

Data Analyst with practical experience in data management, business process automation and AI-powered solutions — building the full chain from raw extract to governed model to a dashboard a non-technical manager can actually read.

Day to day: Python (Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn) for analysis, SQL for modelling, Power Query & Power Pivot for repeatable ETL and DAX models, and Power BI, Tableau & Looker Studio for the visual layer — with Excel still doing the heavy lifting where it is the fastest tool in the room.

Alongside the technical work, I lead data operations for volunteer programs across Egyptian universities — centralized databases, automated reporting and dashboards used by leadership for real-time decisions.

Field note Operational problems rarely need more data. They need the right model, the right chart, and one automated flow nobody has to babysit.
